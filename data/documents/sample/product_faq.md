# Nimbus Smart Thermostat – Frequently Asked Questions

*Fictional product by Northwind Labs, for demo purposes.*

## General

**What is the Nimbus Smart Thermostat?**
Nimbus is a smart thermostat for radiators. It learns your daily routine and heats only
when you need it. On average, customers save **up to 23% heating costs** per year.

**How much does it cost?**
The Nimbus Starter Kit (1 thermostat + 1 bridge) costs **129 EUR**.
Additional thermostats cost **59 EUR** each.

**Which radiator valves are supported?**
Nimbus fits valves with an M30 x 1.5 thread (most common in Germany). Adapters for
Danfoss RA, RAV and RAVL valves are included in the box.

## Setup

**How do I install Nimbus?**
1. Install the Nimbus app (iOS 16+ or Android 11+).
2. Plug the bridge into a power socket near your Wi-Fi router.
3. Screw the thermostat onto the radiator valve.
4. Press the button on the thermostat for 3 seconds until the LED blinks blue.
The setup takes about 10 minutes per room.

**Does Nimbus need Wi-Fi?**
Only the bridge needs Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz). The thermostats talk to the bridge via Zigbee.
One bridge supports up to **32 thermostats**.

## Battery and hardware

**What batteries does Nimbus use?**
Two AA batteries. They last about **2 years**. The app warns you when the battery level
is below 15%.

**What is the temperature range?**
You can set temperatures from 5 °C to 30 °C in steps of 0.5 °C.

## Warranty and support

**How long is the warranty?**
Nimbus has a **3-year warranty**. You can return it within 30 days for a full refund.

**Does Nimbus work without internet?**
Yes. Schedules are stored on the thermostat. Without internet, only remote control via the
app does not work.
