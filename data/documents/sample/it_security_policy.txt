NORTHWIND LABS GMBH - IT SECURITY POLICY (fictional, for demo purposes)
Version 3.0, valid from February 2026

1. Passwords
Passwords must have at least 14 characters.
Use the company password manager "Vaultly" for all work passwords.
Never reuse a work password for private accounts.
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for email, GitHub and the HR portal.

2. Devices
Company laptops must use full disk encryption (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows).
The screen must lock automatically after 5 minutes without activity.
Install updates within 7 days after they are released.
Private USB sticks are not allowed on company laptops.

3. Phishing and incidents
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links.
Report it with the "Report phishing" button in Outlook.
A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour (extension 4242).
Security incidents are handled by the security team: security@northwind-labs.example

4. Data classification
Public: marketing material, website texts.
Internal: handbook, meeting notes.
Confidential: customer data, salaries, contracts. Confidential data must never be uploaded
to public AI tools or cloud services that are not approved by IT.
