# Northwind Labs GmbH – Employee Handbook

*Fictional company for demo purposes. Last updated: January 2026.*

Northwind Labs GmbH is a software company based in Hamburg, Germany. We build tools for
smart buildings. The company was founded in 2017 and has 85 employees.

## 1. Working hours

- The standard working time is **38 hours per week**, Monday to Friday.
- **Core hours are from 10:00 to 15:00.** Everyone should be available in this time.
- Outside the core hours you can choose when you start and end your day (flexitime).
- Overtime is recorded in the TimeTrack app. Up to 40 hours of overtime can be
  carried over to the next month. More overtime must be approved by your team lead.

## 2. Vacation

- Every full-time employee gets **28 vacation days per year**.
- After 5 years at Northwind Labs you get 2 extra days (30 days in total).
- Request vacation in the HR portal at least 3 weeks in advance for absences longer than 5 days.
- Unused vacation days can be carried over, but they **expire on 31 March** of the next year.
- 24 December and 31 December are additional days off for everyone.

## 3. Remote work (home office)

- Employees can work from home up to **3 days per week**.
- Tuesday is our **team day**: everyone works in the Hamburg office.
- Working remotely from another EU country is allowed for up to 20 days per year.
  Tell HR at least 2 weeks before the trip.
- Every employee gets a one-time **home office budget of 600 EUR** for a desk, chair or monitor.

## 4. Sick leave

- If you are sick, inform your team lead before 9:30 on the same day.
- From the **third day** of sickness you need a doctor's note (AU certificate).

## 5. Benefits

- Public transport ticket (Deutschlandticket) paid 100% by the company.
- **Learning budget: 1,500 EUR per year** for courses, books and conferences.
- Free lunch in the office every Tuesday (team day).
- Company pension plan: Northwind Labs adds 20% to your own contribution.
